Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect your name, email address, company name (optional), project type, estimated budget, and message. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

How it's processed

Form submissions are handled by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form processing service. Your data is transmitted securely and stored temporarily by Formspree to enable email delivery. Formspree's privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to any third party beyond Formspree for the purposes described above. We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit consent. We do not use cookies or tracking scripts.

Your rights

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data submitted through this form. To do so, contact cocoaeditingservices@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email cocoaeditingservices@gmail.com.
